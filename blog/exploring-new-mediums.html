Exploring New Mediums

March 1, 2024

For most of last year I stayed close to digital illustration — it's fast, forgiving, and easy to iterate on. But staying inside one medium for too long starts to feel like repeating the same sentence in different fonts. So this month I gave myself permission to be bad at something new on purpose.

I started with fabric. Fashion sketching turned out to be a completely different kind of drawing than illustration — you're not just drawing a shape, you're drawing a shape that has to fall, fold, and move the way real fabric does. My first few sketches looked stiff, like the dress was made of cardboard. It took a while to loosen up and let the lines suggest movement instead of trying to render every fold.

Photography was the opposite problem. Instead of building an image from nothing, I had to learn to see what was already there — waiting for the right light, the right angle, the right moment when a room feels like itself. No amount of editing afterward fixes a photo taken at the wrong time of day.

What surprised me most is how much these new mediums fed back into my illustration work. The fabric studies taught me more about drapery than any tutorial. The photography taught me to be more patient with lighting in digital scenes. Mixing mediums, even clumsily, seems to be the fastest way to get better at any one of them.
